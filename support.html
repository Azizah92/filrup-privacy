Quick start

The four things most people do first.

Log fuel or an expense by voice

On Home, tap Tap to speak and say it the way you would tell a friend: “$42 at QuikTrip, 11 gallons” or “$21 software subscription”. FILRUP fills in the details and files it to the right vehicle and job — or tap Type it instead.

Track your miles

Open Miles and log a trip or your whole day, then pick the job it was for. Business miles build your IRS deduction estimate; personal miles stay separate and never count.

Renewals & documents

Add your vehicle’s insurance, registration and emissions dates in Vehicles & documents. FILRUP reminds you before each one is due and keeps photos of the documents behind Face ID.

Your tax packet

In Taxes → Tax packet, choose what to include and tap Send to my tax pro. Your year goes out as a PDF and an Excel file with live formulas.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Getting started

How do I log something by voice?

On Home (or the Miles and Expenses tabs), tap Tap to speak and say what happened — “$38.50 regular at Fry’s”, “12 miles to a showing, real estate”, “$65 lunch with a client”. FILRUP shows what it heard before anything is saved. If you don’t name a job, it asks you to pick one.

Can I type instead?

Yes. Tap Type it next to the mic and write the same sentence, or open any form and fill in the fields yourself.

Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The whole app works in English and Spanish, and you can speak or type in either. Switch with the EN / ES button at the top of Home.

Can I track more than one vehicle?

Yes, as many as you like. Each vehicle keeps its own fuel log, miles, service history, renewals and documents. Tap Change on Home to switch.

Miles & taxes

How is the deduction estimated?

Business miles × the IRS standard mileage rate for the year (76¢ a mile for 2026). See the rates on irs.gov. It’s an estimate for your records, not tax advice — confirm with your tax professional.

What’s business vs personal?

Business miles are driving you do for work — to a showing, a delivery, a client or a job site — and you tag them to that job. Commuting and errands are personal. Personal miles are kept in your log but never count toward the deduction.

How do I send my tax packet to my accountant?

Go to Taxes → Tax packet, choose what to include (miles, fuel, expenses, service, receipts), add your tax pro’s email once, and tap Send to my tax pro. They get a PDF and an Excel file with live formulas.

Can I import from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Settings → Import from spreadsheet reads a CSV of expenses, miles or fuel. FILRUP shows a preview of the columns before anything is added.

Fuel & expenses

Why does it ask for my location?

Only to look up the fuel station you’re at, so its name and address fill in for you when you log a fill-up. You can always type the station yourself, and the app works fine without location access.

Are receipts required?

No. A receipt photo is optional on every fill-up and expense. If you add one, it goes into your tax packet with the entry.

Can I log a personal expense?

Yes. Pick Personal as the job. It stays in your history but is left out of your deduction and your tax packet.

Renewals

When do reminders arrive?

30 days, 7 days and 1 day before each renewal is due, and on the day itself — each at 9 AM.

Where are my documents stored?

On your phone, behind Face ID (or your passcode). Document photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Automatic trips (coming soon)

What will auto-tracking do?

When you turn it on, FILRUP will notice when you start driving, record the trip start to finish, and ask you which job it was for when you park. You confirm every trip — nothing counts until you do.

Will it drain my battery?

It’s built to be light: it uses the phone’s motion sensors to tell when you’re driving and only uses GPS during a drive, then goes quiet again when you stop.

Can I turn it off?

Anytime, with one switch in Settings or on the Miles tab. Trips you already saved stay saved.

Do you see where I drive?

No. Your trips and their routes stay on your phone.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. FILRUP has no account to sign into, and your entries aren’t kept on our servers.

How do I back up?

FILRUP saves a backup on your phone automatically, and it’s included in your phone’s iCloud or Google backup. You can also tap Settings → Backup & restore → Back up now to save a copy to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. Restoring only adds entries back — it never deletes or replaces anything.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all my data. FILRUP asks twice before it erases anything, because it can’t be undone — back up first if you might need it.

Billing

What does FILRUP cost?

$1.99 a month, or the Family plan at $3.99 a month for up to 6 drivers. Automatic trips will be an optional plan with a 14-day free trial.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple and Google. iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → FILRUP. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → FILRUP. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Family plan — how does it work?

One person subscribes to Family and gets a family code in Settings. Up to 5 others enter it in their own FILRUP (Settings → Join a Family, or “Have a family code?” on the paywall). Everyone gets full access, and each person’s data stays private on their own phone.
