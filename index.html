Log My Day

On the road all day? Speak your starting odometer when you head out and your ending odometer when you're done — FILRUP does the math and saves your day as business or personal miles — with a gentle evening nudge if you forget to close out. Perfect for rideshare and delivery drivers — Uber, Lyft, DoorDash, Instacart, Shipt, and Amazon Flex.

Log a Trip

Meeting a client across town? Log a single trip by entering miles directly, or start and end odometer readings — FILRUP calculates the distance and tags the purpose. Perfect for realtors, self-employed contractors, off-duty police & private-security pros, and other 1099 drivers.
